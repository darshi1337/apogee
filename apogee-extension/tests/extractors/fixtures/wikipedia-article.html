Coastal ecosystem
Not to be confused with Salt pan.
Area 22,000 km2

A salt marsh is a coastal ecosystem in the upper intertidal zone.[1] It is dominated by halophytic plants.[2]

Salt marshes play a large role in the aquatic food web and in the delivery of nutrients to coastal waters.

Geomorphology

edit

Salt marshes occur on low-energy shorelines where fine sediment can settle out of suspension.

Tidal flooding

Marsh surfaces are flooded and drained twice daily by the tides.

Ecology

  • Cordgrass dominates the lower marsh.
  • Saltmeadow rush appears higher up the elevation gradient.
