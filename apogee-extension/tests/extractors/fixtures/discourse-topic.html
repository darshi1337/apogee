We are compiling C++ modules to WebAssembly using Emscripten and our .wasm binary is around 5MB. What compiler flags or optimization tools (like twiggy or binaryen) do you recommend?
eve
12
Use `-Oz` optimization in Emscripten and run `wasm-opt -O4` from Binaryen. That usually drops binary size by 40-50%.
